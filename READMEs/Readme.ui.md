The colored bar under the number indicates your heart rate zone.

![alt text](./image.png)

As a reference, here is what each color bar means.

**This is not training advice — use at your own risk.**

- Nothing: heart rate below 50%
- 1: 50%–60%, very light
- 2: 60%–70%, light
- 3: 70%–80%, moderate
- 4: 80%–90%, hard
- 5: above 90%, maximum

This mod supports DIY interfaces via Unity asset bundles. See [UnityUI/Readme.md](../UnityUI/Readme.md) for details.
